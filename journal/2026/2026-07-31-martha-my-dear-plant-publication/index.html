Martha My Dear Plant Publication

Summary

Martha My Dear now has a complete Orchid Rescue profile covering her July 19 rescue, naturally shed oldest leaf, visible root condition, first soak, and planned post-bloom repot.

Accomplishments

  • Built and validated Martha’s canonical Plant Model workspace.
  • Published all six supplied photographs as a dated rescue timeline.
  • Added accurate hero, current-image, status, and acquisition metadata.
  • Removed Golden Slumbers from the orchid collection.
  • Added an optional canonical page description to the Plant Model so healthy markdown rescues are not mislabeled as neglected plants.

Lessons Learned

  • A plant-page description needs an authoritative override because the shared rescue wording does not accurately describe every acquisition.
  • A small photo set can preserve every meaningful view without making the timeline feel repetitive.

Next Steps

  • Repot Martha after the current flowers fade and document whether a nursery plug is hidden in the root ball.
