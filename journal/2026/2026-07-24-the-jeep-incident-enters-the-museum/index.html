The Jeep Incident Enters the Museum

Summary

At 15, I found a Jeep and enough mud to bury it. Decades later, I found the photographs.

The Museum of Dumb Ideas exhibit now documents an early demonstration of confidence exceeding experience, the discovery that four-wheel drive is not a recovery plan, and the outside assistance required when the original theory stopped moving.

Accomplishments

  • Added a full museum-style exhibit with accession number MDI-0002.
  • Preserved the two surviving photographs as exhibit artifacts.
  • Updated the Museum index to place the Jeep Incident on permanent display.
  • Extended the Museum styling with a reusable artifact gallery.

Lessons Learned

Confidence is not traction.

This conclusion was available in the 1980s, but apparently required a full-scale field experiment.
