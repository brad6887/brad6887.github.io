Lady Madonna Plant Publication

Summary

Lady Madonna now has a complete Orchid Rescue page built from her exported care log, photographs, and photo sidecars. The record begins in January, follows the flower spike into an April bloom, documents the setback caused by the June move, and ends with strong July root growth and what may be the start of a new leaf.

Accomplishments

  • Built the canonical Plant Model workspace and preserved the complete supplied source package.
  • Kept the remembered $5 Lowe’s clearance purchase in late 2025 explicitly uncertain.
  • Published a verified January-through-July timeline with 19 selected photos.
  • Passed Abbey plant validation, publication, and the 115-page production site build.

Lessons Learned

  • Missing numbered photographs should be recorded as evidence gaps when their sidecars are present.
  • A recovery can happen twice: first in the flowers, then underground after a move.

Next Steps
