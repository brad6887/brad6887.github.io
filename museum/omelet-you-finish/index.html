Accession No. MDI-0001

OmeletYouFinish.com

Imma let you finish, but this may be one of the greatest unused domains of all time.

Exhibit Information

Classification

Original Concept
Omelet-themed travel blog
Current Status
Retired before construction; breakfast research continues
Primary Artifact
One completely legitimate domain registration and several actual omelets
Cause of Failure
Five additional minutes of thought
Curator's Position
Omelets are excellent; the domain remains difficult to defend

Description

OmeletYouFinish.com began with a simple observation: people enjoy breakfast, people enjoy travel, and the internet had somehow failed to combine those interests with a Kanye West reference.

The proposed site would document omelets encountered while traveling. Each breakfast would become a destination, each restaurant a cultural experience, and each plate of eggs another opportunity to justify purchasing the domain.

To be clear, the problem was never the omelets. I love omelets. The domain was secured because the subject was worthy, the joke was available, and domain registrars do not require a cooling-off period. Development, fortunately, did not begin.

Original Hypothesis

People love breakfast. People love travel. This obviously combines both.

The Omelet Philosophy

A good omelet does not need a concept meeting. Keep it simple, cook it well, and let eggs be eggs. Add good fillings with some restraint. Do not put pancake batter or other gimmicks into the eggs in an attempt to improve a thing that was already working.

Waffle House makes a perfect omelet. This is not faint praise or nostalgia. It is the standard: light, properly cooked, and still recognizably an omelet.

Mixing a pile of random ingredients into eggs and cooking the entire arrangement to death does not produce an omelet. It produces a hotel or cruise-ship buffet warning. The presence of a chafing dish does not change the definition.

What Went Wrong

Nothing technically went wrong. The idea simply encountered the rare but effective project-management technique known as thinking about it again.

Further review determined that maintaining an international omelet database might require more breakfast travel than the available budget could support.

The philosophy survived. The business plan did not. This is probably the correct outcome: eat the omelet while it is hot, and do not turn every sincere enthusiasm into a content strategy.

Curator's Note

I stand by the omelets. I do not necessarily stand by buying a domain every time I think of a joke. Sometimes buying the domain is enough.

Lessons Learned

  • Domains are cheaper than fully developed bad ideas.
  • Not every joke deserves a production server.
  • Keep omelets simple and cook them with care.
  • Waffle House has already solved this problem.
  • A failed project can still become a successful story.
